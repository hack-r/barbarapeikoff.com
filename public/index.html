The Passion of Ayn Rand

Biography · Doubleday, 1986

Barbara Branden knew Ayn Rand for nearly two decades and conducted more than two hundred interviews for this life of a woman who was neither goddess nor sinner, but a human being. Adapted as a Showtime film and, for the stage, a play by Julian Barry.

Principles of Efficient Thinking

Ten-lecture course · MP3 download

Thinking is an acquired skill, and like every human skill it involves principles that have to be identified and learned. This course covers the theory of efficient thinking (focusing, the subconscious, the conceptual level, integration) and the practical techniques that avoid thinking errors and maximize the productiveness of mental effort.

Feminist Interpretations of Ayn Rand

Anthology · Penn State Press, 1999

The first volume to engage Ayn Rand's thought from feminist perspectives, edited by Mimi Reisel Gladstein and Chris Matthew Sciabarra. Barbara's lead essay, “Ayn Rand: The Reluctant Feminist,” views Rand's life as a feminist manifesto while focusing on the aspects of her life and thought that contradicted feminism.

About Barbara

Barbara is a writer and lecturer. She earned her M.A. in philosophy at New York University, with a thesis on free will written under Sidney Hook. She was Ayn Rand's intimate friend and colleague for eighteen years, Managing Editor of The Objectivist, and Executive Vice-President of the Nathaniel Branden Institute, where she created the course on efficient thinking.
